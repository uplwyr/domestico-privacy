Domestico ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile application that helps household members manage shared chores, shopping lists, and pantry/inventory tracking together. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and what rights you have.

1. Who We Are

This app is developed by UP LYR. If you have any questions, you can reach us at uplwyr@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account.
  • User content: The tasks, shopping lists, pantry/inventory items, and shopping history (receipts) you create.
  • Household information: The invite code and membership details of the household you are connected to.
  • Notification preferences: Your chosen reminder times and whether notifications are enabled.
  • Technical data: Basic technical information automatically processed by our Firebase infrastructure to keep the app functioning properly (e.g. a device notification token).

We do not collect sensitive data categories such as location, payment information, or health data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To synchronize your tasks, shopping list, and inventory data in real time with the household members you are connected to.
  • To send reminder notifications at the times you choose.
  • To let you sign in securely and manage your household connection.

Your data is never used for advertising and is never sold or rented to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

Your content (tasks, shopping lists, inventory items) is only visible to the household members you connect with via invite code. Your data is stored and processed using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which has its own privacy policy governing that infrastructure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. From within the app, you can:

  • Leave a household to end your access to that household's shared data.
  • Sign out of your account.
  • Request full deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at uplwyr@gmail.com. We will fulfill such requests within a reasonable time (no later than 30 days).

6. Permissions

The app requests notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) from your device in order to show reminder notifications. You can revoke this permission at any time from your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Domestico is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from users in this age group.

8. Data Security

Your data is transmitted securely over HTTPS via the Google Firebase infrastructure. Household data can only be accessed by authenticated users who are members of that household.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you within the app or via this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about privacy, please contact: uplwyr@gmail.com
